# TECHNOLOGY PLATFORM & SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT

**THIS TECHNOLOGY PLATFORM & SOFTWARE LICENSING AGREEMENT** (the *"Agreement"*) is made and entered into as of this _____ day of ________________, 2026 (the *"Effective Date"*), by and between:

### PARTIES:

1. **PRAISE IBE** (hereinafter referred to as the **"Service Provider"** or **"Developer"**), a Software Engineer and Technology Consultant operating in Nigeria; and
2. **ZAPOLS RESTAURANT & LOUNGE** (hereinafter referred to as the **"Client"** or **"Restaurant"**), an operating hospitality and food service business.

*(The Service Provider and the Client are hereinafter individually referred to as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties".)*

---

## RECITALS

**WHEREAS**, the Client operates a restaurant and office meal delivery business and seeks a reliable, custom-built digital ordering system to streamline food orders, eliminate delivery errors, and track kitchen preparations;

**WHEREAS**, the Service Provider has engineered, deployed, and maintains a proprietary digital ordering platform known as the **"Zapols Ordering & Kitchen Verification System"** (the *"Platform"*), which includes mobile web ordering, WhatsApp ticket formatting, secure 4-digit pickup code handover verification, and real-time cloud database syncing;

**WHEREAS**, the Client desires to license and utilize the Platform for its operations, and the Service Provider agrees to license, host, and maintain the Platform under a transactional service-charge remuneration model;

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the mutual promises, covenants, and valuable consideration contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:

---

## 1. SCOPE OF SERVICES & PLATFORM LICENSING

1.1. **Platform License:** The Service Provider grants the Client a non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable license to utilize the Zapols Ordering & Kitchen Verification System for the duration of this Agreement.  
1.2. **Components Provided:** The system provided includes:
   - Mobile-first, responsive customer menu and cart interface;
   - WhatsApp order ticketing generation with automated customer breakdowns;
   - Unique 4-digit pickup code security verification engine;
   - Real-time Kitchen Order Board for packing and handover verification;
   - Cloud database infrastructure and synchronization (Supabase integration);
   - Vendor admin controls for menu items, pricing, and live stock availability.  
1.3. **Technical Support & Maintenance:** The Service Provider agrees to provide ongoing software maintenance, critical bug fixes, server uptime monitoring, and routine system updates.

---

## 2. COMMERCIAL TERMS & SERVICE FEE STRUCTURE

2.1. **Service Fee Per Order:** In consideration for the development, hosting, continuous maintenance, and technical operation of the Platform, the Service Provider shall be entitled to a fee of **₦100 (One Hundred Naira only)** per completed order processed through the Platform (the *"Platform Service Fee"*).  
2.2. **Customer Service Charge Billing:** The Platform Service Fee is embedded directly into the customer's order calculation as the standard **"Service charge — ₦100"** paid by the customer at checkout.  
2.3. **Beneficial Ownership of Service Charge:** The Client explicitly acknowledges and agrees that the ₦100 Service Charge collected from customers is the exclusive property of the Service Provider and does not constitute restaurant food revenue.  
2.4. **Takeaway Pack Fees:** For the avoidance of doubt, the standard packaging fee (e.g. ₦300 Takeaway pack) and food totals remain 100% the property and revenue of the Client.

---

## 3. RECONCILIATION, REPORTING & SETTLEMENT

3.1. **Order Record Accounting:** Completed orders shall be tracked, logged, and audited through the system's database logs and kitchen handover records.  
3.2. **Settlement Frequency:** Reconciliation of accrued Service Fees shall occur on a **[Weekly / Monthly]** basis:
   - **Weekly Settlement Option:** Every Monday for all orders completed in the preceding 7 calendar days; OR
   - **Monthly Settlement Option:** Within three (3) business days following the end of each calendar month.  
3.3. **Settlement Formula:**  
   $$\text{Total Remittance Due} = \text{Total Verified Completed Orders} \times \text{₦100}$$  
3.4. **Remittance Account:** The Client shall remit all accrued Platform Service Fees to the Service Provider’s nominated bank account:
   - **Bank Name:** Moniepoint Microfinance Bank
   - **Account Number:** 7082291264
   - **Account Name:** Praise Ibe  
   *(or any updated account details provided in writing by the Service Provider).*

---

## 4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY & DATA RIGHTS

4.1. **Software Ownership:** The Service Provider retains all exclusive rights, title, copyright, source code, trade secrets, and intellectual property in and to the Platform, including any customizations, algorithms, or enhancements. Nothing in this Agreement transfers software copyright to the Client.  
4.2. **Client Content & Branding:** The Client retains sole ownership of its business name, trademarks, logos, menu recipes, food photographs, and customer relationships.  
4.3. **Data Protection:** Customer contact details collected through orders shall be held confidentially and shall not be sold, transferred, or exploited for unauthorized third-party commercial marketing.

---

## 5. SERVICE LEVEL COMMITMENT & OBLIGATIONS

5.1. **Service Provider Obligations:**
   - Maintain Platform availability with a minimum target of 98.5% uptime during business hours;
   - Promptly rectify any reported technical defects or glitches impacting order placement;
   - Ensure secure cloud database connectivity and WhatsApp compatibility.  
5.2. **Client Obligations:**
   - Maintain active internet connectivity on kitchen receiving devices;
   - Ensure kitchen staff verify customer 4-digit pickup codes diligently before food handover;
   - Promptly remit all accumulated ₦100 Platform Service Fees in accordance with Section 3.

---

## 6. TERM & TERMINATION

6.1. **Initial Term:** This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and remain in full force and effect for an initial period of **twelve (12) months** (the *"Initial Term"*).  
6.2. **Automatic Renewal:** Upon expiration of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive six (6) month periods unless either Party provides written notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days prior to the expiration date.  
6.3. **Termination for Cause:** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately if:
   - The other Party commits a material breach of this Agreement (including non-remittance of fees) and fails to cure such breach within fourteen (14) calendar days of receiving written notice;
   - Either Party ceases business operations or becomes insolvent.  
6.4. **Settlement Upon Termination:** Upon termination, all unpaid Service Fees accrued up to the effective termination date shall immediately become due and payable within five (5) business days.
